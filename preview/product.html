Product Details

The Oversized Essential Tee is FOLDCUT's foundational piece — designed for the person who understands that the best fashion doesn't shout. It speaks through fit, fabric, and intention.

  • 240gsm premium ring-spun cotton
  • Dropped shoulder silhouette
  • Relaxed oversized fit (size down for a regular fit)
  • Ribbed crewneck collar
  • Double-stitched hem for durability
  • FOLDCUT woven label at hem
  • Available in Black, Off White, Camel
  • Unisex — fits true to size on women as a cropped oversized look

Material Composition

  • 100% Premium Ring-Spun Cotton (240gsm)
  • Pre-shrunk fabric — will not shrink after washing
  • OEKO-TEX® certified materials

Care Instructions

  • Machine wash cold (30°C) — gentle cycle
  • Do not bleach
  • Tumble dry low or air dry
  • Cool iron if needed — do not iron on print
  • Do not dry clean

For best results, wash inside out and air dry. This preserves the fabric texture and the deep black dye for longer.
